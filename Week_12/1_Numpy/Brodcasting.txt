============================================================
BROADCASTING RULE 1
============================================================

Compare dimensions from RIGHT to LEFT.


Example:

(4, 3)
(3,)

Think of (3,) as:

(1, 3)

Now:

(4, 3)
(1, 3)

3 == 3

Therefore compatible.

============================================================
BROADCASTING RULE 2
============================================================

Two dimensions are compatible when:

1. They are equal

OR

2. One of them is 1

(3, 1)
(1, 3)

      ↓

Both dimensions can be expanded.

Therefore compatible.

(3, 4)
(4, 3)

Compare from right:

4 != 3
3 != 4

Therefore NOT compatible.
